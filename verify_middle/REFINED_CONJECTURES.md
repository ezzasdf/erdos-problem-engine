# Refined Conjectures: the Middle-Digit and First-2 Structure of 2ⁿ in Ternary

Self-contained statement of the conjectures distilled from `verify_middle/FINDINGS.md`
(Phase 7 M1) and `verify_middle/PHASE8_FINDINGS.md` (Phase 8), with explicit
epistemic labeling of every claim. Target: the Erdős ternary conjecture — no `2ⁿ`
whose ternary expansion uses only the digits `{0, 1}`.

## Definitions

- `L(n) = ⌊n·log₃2⌋ + 1` — the number of ternary digits of `2ⁿ`.
- `W = ⌊L/3⌋` — the window length. Digit positions are least-significant-first;
  the ternary expansion splits into the trailing `[0, W)`, middle `[W, ⌊2L/3⌋)`,
  and leading `[⌊2L/3⌋, L)` blocks.
- `f(n)` — the position of the *first* digit 2 in `2ⁿ` counting from the most
  significant digit (0-indexed), or `L(n)` if `2ⁿ` has no digit 2.
- Datasets (both existing and verified):
  - `verify_middle/results_m1.csv` — 999,992 rows, `n = 9 … 10⁶`, window statistics
    (columns `n,L,W,<t|m|l>_freq,_long,_free,_first,t0,t1,m0,m1,l0`).
  - `verify_middle/first2_records.csv` — 19 first-2-position record-holders over
    `n = 0 … 10⁹` (columns `n,f,L,ratio,prefix,mantissa_precision,record_number,gap_from_previous_record`).

## Status table

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Proved** | Proven result with a rigorous argument (e.g. the exact `P(f ≥ L)` law and the counting-identity upper bounds from Phase 8 findings). |
| **Empirical** | Measured exactly over the stated dataset range (e.g. `results_m1.csv`, `first2_records.csv`). |
| **Conjectural** | Extrapolated beyond the measured range; not proved; falsifiable by a single counterexample. |

## M4-C — middle-window digit-2 lower bound (conjectural)

For every `n > 8`, the middle third `[⌊L/3⌋, ⌊2L/3⌋)` of the ternary expansion of
`2ⁿ` contains a digit 2; more precisely, the digit-2 frequency in that window is
`≥ 0.246` for `n > 1000`.

> The threshold `n > 1000` and the constant `0.246` are **conjectural extrapolations** from the existing dataset (`n ≤ 10⁶`, `verify_middle/results_m1.csv`). They are **not proved results.** The measured facts are: the only n with a 2-free middle window over `9 ≤ n ≤ 10⁶` are `n ∈ {14, 24, 76}`, and the minimum middle-window digit-2 frequency over `1000 < n ≤ 10⁶` is `0.246192893` at `n = 1874`.

## M4-C2 — two-sided independence (empirical + conjectural)

The digit-2 frequencies of the trailing, middle and leading thirds of `2ⁿ` are
statistically independent across `n` (Spearman `|ρ| < 0.005` over `n ≤ 10⁶`), and
boundary digits pair near-uniformly. **This is a measured (empirical) fact over
`n ≤ 10⁶`; the extrapolation to all `n` is conjectural and not proved.**

Measured supports (see Evidence):

- Spearman correlations `t_freq×m_freq = 0.0011`, `l_freq×m_freq = −0.0030`,
  `l_freq×t_freq = −0.0000` — effectively zero.
- Cross-tabulated boundary digit pairs `t0×m0`, `t1×m0`, `m0×l0` near-uniform;
  chi-square 1.1, 2.3, 7.6 — no evidence of dependence. (`t0` never equals 0 since
  `2ⁿ ≢ 0 (mod 3)`.)

## M4-C3 — first-2-position linear bound (conjectural)

For every `n ≥ 1`, `f(n) ≤ 3.5·log₃ n`, i.e. `2ⁿ` has a digit 2 among its first
`⌈3.5·log₃ n⌉` ternary digits. **This is a conjecture supported by all tested data
(`n ≤ 10⁹`), not a theorem.**

- Sharpest tested point: `n = 121` with `f(121)/log₃(121) ≈ 3.437` — a 2% margin
  below `3.5` (the `f/log₃ n` maximum over all 19 records).
- The ratio decays toward ≈ 2.8 for large `n` (the `log_{3/2}` slope); the last
  record (`n = 464,263,536`) has `f/L ≈ 1.7×10⁻⁷`.
- The 19 measured records obey `f ≈ log_{3/2} n`; least-squares fit
  `f ≈ 1.047·log_{3/2} n + 0.521` over `n > 0` (slope ≈ 1.04).
- Probability heuristic: `P(f(n) ≥ L) = C·(2/3)^L` with `C = 1.1148` (Phase 8,
  proved law for the *measure*), predicts `max f(N) ≈ log_{3/2}(N·C)`; the observed
  maxima (36, 40, 50 at `N = 10⁶, 10⁷, 10⁹`) agree within ±2 — but this only
  certifies the records up to `n ≤ 10⁹` and gives **no pointwise theorem** beyond.

## Falsification criteria

| Conjecture | Refuted by |
|---|---|
| **M4-C** | any `n > 76` whose middle window contains no digit 2; or any `n > 1000` with middle-window digit-2 frequency `< 0.246` |
| **M4-C2** | a statistically significant dependence between any pair of window frequencies over a tested range |
| **M4-C3** | any `n` with `f(n) > 3.5·log₃ n` |

A single counterexample at any tested or future `n` refutes the corresponding
conjectural claim. Proved results (e.g. the exact `P(f ≥ L)` law and the counting-
identity upper bounds) are immune.

## Relation to Erdős / Phase 9

- **M4-C vs the Erdős conjecture.** The middle-window bound is the Erdős conjecture
  restricted to the middle block: a proof of M4-C would prove the full conjecture
  for all `n > 8` (the base cases `n ≤ 8` are already checked).
- **M4-C2 and the two-sided search.** The Phase 9 two-sided search treats the leading
  and trailing blocks as independent inputs; M4-C2 justifies that modelling over the
  measured range `n ≤ 10⁶`. The extrapolation beyond is conjectural.
- **M4-C3 and certification.** If proved, `f(n) ≤ C·log₃ n` is a genuinely new
  pointwise theorem on the digits of `2ⁿ` (stronger than density-zero statements)
  and — combined with Saye's trailing-digit pruning — a two-sided search could
  certify far beyond `5.9×10²¹`. The Phase 9 program (exact orbit counting via
  Ostrowski / three-distance arithmetic) is the honest frontier left open; the
  records `n ≤ 10⁹` are the only pointwise statement currently certified.
